Source: the GO trading workbook (sheets 2024–2027), kept in the private vcom repository (data/go/tokaj_go_sales.csv). The workbook calls the sites ASTROPUT = Putnok, ASTROSER = Serenyfalva, ASTROKAZ = Sajokaza, ASTROSZU = Szuhakálló 1, ASTROSZELES = Szuhakálló 2 (checked against the metered volumes of 2024 and 2025). Status: Delivered = delivery date is in the past and the contract was not re-dated (assumed, the workbook has no delivery confirmation); Re-dated = the workbook has a “Delivery to be amended” date. The AXPO contract for Q1–Q2 2026 is 64,000 MWh in total; only 9,000 MWh of it stays on 2026-11-30 and 55,000 MWh moved to 2027-11-30 (the per-site split is not in the workbook, so each row shows the whole contract volume). The 2nd March 2025 tranche uses the actually traded volume (column “实际交易电量”), as the workbook’s own totals do.

Produced comes from the meter readings (/ops/api/metered, hourly files, summed per month) and includes grid curtailment. The GO actually issued by the registry can differ slightly (it is based on the registry’s own settled meter data). 2024 counts August–December only, because that is what was sold in 2024; use the checkbox to include Putnok’s and Serenyfalva’s earlier months (Mar–Jul 2024). Meter data ends at the date shown in the status line; later months of the current year are only estimates.
