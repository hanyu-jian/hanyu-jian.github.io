Theoretical production vs production forecast

Unit: kWh per interval (15 min or 1 h), Budapest local time, interval start. Deviation = production forecast − theoretical production (positive = forecast too high).
Production forecast: DA is the base forecast. IDA1, IDA2, IDA3 and ID3 are successive revisions of it; where a revision has no value for an interval, the previous version carries forward. The orange line is DA with every revision up to the selected one applied (default: latest).

Summary by site (selected period)

Theoretical production is calculated from VCOM POA irradiance (includes the AC limit, excludes grid curtailment); it is not the meter reading. Statistics only use intervals where theoretical production and the respective forecast are both available. “Abs. error” = Σ|forecast − theoretical| / Σtheoretical.
