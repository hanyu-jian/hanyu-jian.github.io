How each 15-minute interval is settled (the version confirmed against the Robestec workbook, simplified to a single imbalance price):
H = DA forecast  |  F = final plan = the UST forecast issued lead time minutes before the interval (the smallest available lead time at or above the chosen one; none → F = H)  |  AH = theoretical production (from measured POA, includes the AC limit)
DA revenue = P · H / 1000  |  IDC revenue = AA · (F − H) / 1000  |  Imbalance = F − AH  |  Imbalance fee = −(F − AH) · Pimb / 1000  |  Final result = DA revenue + IDC revenue + imbalance fee
P = day-ahead price, AA = ID3 index price, Pimb = imbalance price (one price for both directions, so the system direction does not change the total), all in EUR/MWh. A positive fee is income (surplus sold at a positive price), a negative fee is a cost. DA-only result = DA revenue − (H − AH) · Pimb / 1000 (no IDC correction); IDC gain = final result − DA-only result (can be negative).
Abs. imbalance % = Σ|F − AH| / ΣAH, compared with the same figure for DA alone (Σ|H − AH| / ΣAH). Only intervals with theoretical production and a DA forecast are counted. A missing price counts as 0 and is listed in the “Price gaps” column (intervals); days without any DA or imbalance price (usually the last day or two) are left out.
Data: DA forecast and theoretical production from the vcom repository; UST from R2 (live > replay > hindcast per cell); prices from the hu-market-data combined table (day-ahead, ID3 and imbalance price in EUR/MWh; the HUF imbalance price before 2026-10-01 is converted with the MNB rate of the previous working day).
