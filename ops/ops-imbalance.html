Sensitivity to the DA bid share

Selected sites and period, for every DA bid share and both ways of handling the rest (EUR)

Rest in IDC: the DA position is D = bid share × DA forecast; the rest is traded in the intraday market so that the final position equals the UST forecast F issued lead time minutes before the interval (F − D is bought or sold at the ID3 price; where no UST forecast exists the position stays at D). Rest in imbalance: nothing is traded after the DA, the position stays at D and the whole difference to the actual production is settled at the imbalance price (an opportunistic imbalance position).
Reading the table: with the prices of the period known, the result is linear in the bid share, so it changes by the same amount for every +10 points and the best share is always 0% or the upper limit; the slope is the volume-weighted spread between the DA price and the price the rest is settled at (ID3 or imbalance). The sign of that spread can change between months (pick a single month above to see it), and it is a look back, not a forecastable rule.
With a 100% bid and the rest left in imbalance this is the plain DA-only reference; with a 100% bid and IDC it is the settlement confirmed against the Robestec workbook.

How each 15-minute interval is settled (single imbalance price, so the system direction does not change the total):
H = DA forecast  |  D = DA bid = share × H  |  F = UST forecast issued lead time minutes before (the smallest available lead at or above the chosen one)  |  AH = theoretical production (from measured POA, includes the AC limit)
Final position = F (rest in IDC and F available) or D (otherwise)  |  DA revenue = P · D / 1000  |  IDC revenue = AA · (final position − D) / 1000  |  Imbalance revenue = −(final position − AH) · Pimb / 1000 (positive = income, negative = cost)  |  Result = DA + IDC + imbalance
P = day-ahead price, AA = ID3 index price, Pimb = imbalance price, EUR/MWh. DA-only (100%) = reference with a 100% bid and nothing traded afterwards. Result = revenue / cost after the imbalance cost (DA + IDC + imbalance revenue); Imbalance % = imbalance revenue / result (negative = the imbalance cost as a share of the result); Abs. imbalance volume % = Σ|final position − AH| / ΣAH. Only intervals with theoretical production and a DA forecast are counted. A missing price counts as 0 and is listed under “Price gaps” (intervals); days with no DA or no imbalance price at all (usually the last day or two) are left out.
Data: DA forecast and theoretical production from the vcom repository; UST from R2 (live > replay > hindcast per cell); prices from the hu-market-data combined table (day-ahead, ID3 and imbalance price in EUR/MWh; the HUF imbalance price before 2026-10-01 is converted with the MNB rate of the previous working day). Changing the dates or the IDC lead time reloads the data; everything else is calculated in the browser.
